The site is a static website hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Legal basis: my legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel states that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which covers the transfer to the USA.

### Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Visitors are told apart by a hash computed from the incoming request, and that visitor session is discarded after 24 hours. Only aggregated statistics remain: pages viewed, referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type. No profile is built and no data is combined across websites. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in knowing which content is read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

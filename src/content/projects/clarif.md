Wholesalers often run on estimated margins: profit is inferred from spreadsheets and manually maintained metrics instead of measured. Clarif.io gives them the actual picture of what the business earns, in one place, without the Excel layer.
- Architected the multi-tenant platform end to end, including tenant isolation and the aggregation pipelines behind the profit metrics.
- Engineered the backend services with **.NET Web API** on **PostgreSQL**.
- Built the data-dense dashboards with **Vue.js**, **Nuxt 3**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

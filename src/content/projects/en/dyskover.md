Schools, organisations, teachers, and students manage their course material in one place and use AI to generate practice questions directly from the content they have published. The first release targets learners with learning difficulties; general audiences follow.
- Designing the AI question-generation flow that turns published course material into exercises adapted to learners with learning difficulties.
- Building the **.NET Core** backend and decoupled **Nuxt 3** portals for institutions, teachers, and students.
